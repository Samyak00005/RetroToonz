# D3 — Homepage & Content Cards

D3 applies the RetroToonz content-first design system to the Homepage below the Hero.

## Completed

- Poster cards keep artwork dominant and use restrained hover feedback.
- Card hover no longer relies on strong lift/glow; artwork movement is limited to a subtle scale.
- Watchlist controls keep practical touch targets and keyboard focus states.
- Homepage content rails share one responsive density system: mobile, tablet, laptop and 8-up desktop.
- Section headings and Show more actions use shared design primitives.
- Rail arrows are quiet utility controls and only appear when scrolling is possible.
- Continue Watching is a landscape playback rail with episode context, progress and time remaining.
- Browse by Category now uses actual show artwork in 16:9 cards instead of decorative icon/glow tiles.
- Selected category results reuse the standard ShowCard component.
- Existing Start Watching and Surprise Me brand CTA designs are intentionally unchanged.

## Design intent

Artwork, titles and playback state are the visual hierarchy. Borders, shadows and motion support interaction without competing with the content.
